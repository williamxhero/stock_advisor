# Non-Trading Market Outlook Protocol

Protocol ID: `NonTradingOutlook-v1`

Scope: an explicitly requested weekend or exchange-holiday market-environment
summary and next-trading-session outlook. This is a manual formal-analysis
occurrence, not a monthly, quarterly, or annual review and never edits the
formal schedule.

The research must anchor the market snapshot to the latest completed XSHG
close, then separate subsequently published events from unverified commentary.
It must identify material weekend or holiday developments, the strongest
counter-evidence, unavailable evidence, and the conditions that would change a
next-session view. Do not write retrospective performance statistics, revise a
past judgment, or present the work as a periodic review.
